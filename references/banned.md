# Banned vocabulary

Machine-checked list, kept in sync with `scripts/slop.mjs`. If the script flags a
word that is correct in context, argue with the word, not the rule: use the
concrete alternative.

## Words

delve, tapestry, testament, vibrant, pivotal, crucial, intricate, meticulous,
bolster, garner, underscore, interplay, multifaceted, nuanced, foster, leverage,
utilize, commence, facilitate, encompass, paramount, groundbreaking, cutting-edge,
game-changer, game-changing, transformative, revolutionize, revolutionise,
disruptive, holistic, seamless, robust, comprehensive, endeavor, endeavour,
aforementioned, harnessing, spearheading, showcasing, highlighting, emphasizing,
enhancing, unprecedented, remarkable, stunning, profound, thought leader,
synergy, value proposition, moving forward, circle back, rest assured,
goes without saying

Spanish: aprovechando, potenciando, revolucionario, sin precedentes,
en el mundo actual, en el mundo de hoy, en resumen, en conclusión,
vale la pena destacar, es importante destacar, sin duda alguna

## Phrases

"in today's X", "let's dive", "at its core", "in the realm of", "here's the thing",
"here's the deal", "without further ado", "in a nutshell", "buckle up",
"the bottom line is", "at the end of the day", "it's worth noting",
"it's important to note", "when it comes to", "this is where",
"not just X but Y", "unlock the power", "take it to the next level",
"streamline your", "supercharge your", "bridge the gap", "move the needle"

Spanish: nada más:, solo eso:, no es solo X sino Y

## Openers

I'd be happy to, Let's dive in, Here's what you need to know, Without further ado,
Honestly?, Real talk, Look,

Overall, In conclusion, Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly, Additionally, Moreover,
Furthermore, Notably, Importantly, Interestingly, Indeed

## Chat residue

"I hope this helps", "hope this helps", "let me know if you want/need",
"I hope this finds you well", "as per my last email", "please don't hesitate"

## Knowledge-limit

"as of my last update", "to my knowledge", "up to my last training update",
"based on available information", "is not publicly available", "likely"
